Kumo Ellis creates tender picture books for young children, inspired by cozy homes, loving families, little feelings, and the quiet magic hidden inside everyday moments.

Latest from Kumo Ellis

Aria’s Brave Little No: A Gentle Safety Story for Little Ones

Aria has a kind heart. She likes to help. She likes to be polite. She likes to do the right thing. But one day, a small request begins to feel not quite right. Aria’s Brave Little No is a gentle picture book about listening to your feelings, understanding boundaries, and finding the courage to say no. Soft, reassuring, and age-appropriate, this story helps little ones learn that kindness is important — and so is feeling safe.
